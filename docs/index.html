Why I built this

I spend a lot of my week editing video. Most of those hours go into cutting flubbed takes, tightening pauses and dropping B-roll in the right spot. None of that is an editorial decision. It just takes time. So I built a bridge that hands that part to AI.

01

Precise

It cuts from what was said, then listens back to every cut and checks a few frames. A clipped word or a cut that is too tight gets fixed before it moves on.

02

Knows the story

It reads what was said, how your timeline is laid out and what is on screen. So it cuts where the story needs it, not just where the silences are.

03

B-roll in the same pass

It puts clips and graphics on the timeline itself, as part of the same edit, not as a separate job at the end.

How it works

An AI that edits without checking just gives you a new pile of mistakes. This one checks after every change.

You

Say what you want, like “cut the bad takes”.

AI helper

Claude Code or Codex reads what people said and plans each cut.

The bridge

Runs on your PC and passes each step to Premiere Pro.

Premiere Pro

Makes the edit on your timeline while you watch.

Then it checks its work. It listens to every cut and looks at a few frames. If something is off, it goes back and fixes it.

Questions

Is it free?+

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license. You can use it, change it and share it.

Is it made by Adobe?+

No. It is an independent project. It uses Adobe’s official way of adding plugins to Premiere Pro.

Will it mess up my project?+

It checks before it changes anything, and every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z. Still, try it on a copy of your project first.

How does the AI know what to cut?+

It looks at three things: the words people said, how your timeline is laid out, and how the edit really sounds and looks. It listens to the audio and checks a few still frames.

Will it stop working when Premiere updates?+

It is built on UXP, Adobe’s current way of adding plugins to Premiere Pro, not on the older CEP system that Adobe plans to retire. When a new Premiere version changes something, the setup window and the bridge tell you.

Does it work on a Mac?+

Not yet. Right now it only works on Windows.

Which AI helpers can use it?+

Claude Code and Codex. Any AI helper that can run commands on your PC can use it too.
